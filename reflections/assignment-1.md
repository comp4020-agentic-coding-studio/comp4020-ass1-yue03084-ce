# Assignment 1

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was that I wasn't satisfied with the first homepage that was
generated. I then discussed with the agent in detail what I wanted, and
confirmed it was feasible: for example a model of a Polaroid camera, simulating
the print being ejected after the shutter is pressed, and more realistic
pictures such as an apple or a leaf rather than plain colour blocks.

When I wrote the requirements for the sound, and when I changed how the cake
looked, I went through the details with the agent before writing the prompt,
because I found this much more efficient than writing prompts and adjusting them
over and over.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This week was the first time I extracted the requirements I kept repeating in my
prompts and put them into CLAUDE.md. But by the time I did that, I had already
repeated them several times. Next time I will write these constraints into the
file as soon as the site has roughly taken shape and I am making lots of small
changes to it.

I want to become someone who, once a project has made some progress, has the
habit of actively summing up what I have learned and turning it into rules.
